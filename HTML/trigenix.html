Features

Built to make
3D creation easy

🖼️
Simple Input

Any photo, any object

Upload a JPEG, PNG or WEBP of any object. No 3D software, no special camera setup — just drag and drop.

📦
GLB Output

Industry-standard .glb

Every model is exported as a binary glTF (.glb) — load it in Blender, Unity, three.js, or view it directly in the browser.

🎨
Vertex Colours

Colours baked from your photo

Pixel colours from your input image are projected onto every vertex of the mesh — no UV maps needed.

🔒
Runs Locally

Everything stays on your machine

The entire pipeline runs on your own hardware via Python. No cloud, no data leaving your device.

🌐
Use Anywhere

Games, AR, web & more

Drop the .glb into a game engine, embed it in three.js, open it in AR Quick Look, or inspect it in the built-in 3D viewer.

Process

How it works

STEP 01
🖼️

Upload a photo

Drop any JPEG, PNG or WEBP. Works with product photos, scans, or casual snapshots.

STEP 02
✂️

Background removal

rembg isolates your subject with a clean alpha mask, removing the background automatically.

STEP 03
🌊

Depth & mesh

DepthAnything estimates per-pixel depth. Zero123++ generates surrounding views. The mesh is carved from a visual hull or reconstructed with TripoSR.

STEP 04
📦

Download your .glb

Colours are baked onto the mesh vertices and the model is exported as a .glb — open it in the built-in viewer or take it anywhere.
